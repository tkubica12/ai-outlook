# Tomlook v0.15.0

This release adds customer context: a customer timeline that brings previous
meetings, notes, tasks, MSX opportunities and milestones, Azure consumption and
related communication together, and feeds a compact version of it into meeting
preparation. These are changes since
[v0.14.0](https://github.com/tkubica12/ai-outlook/releases/tag/v0.14.0).
v0.8.0 and later install it automatically after verification when you choose
**Restart now** in Settings → Updates or exit Tomlook.

## Customers in your meetings

Meetings with external participants show **Customers in this meeting** in their
details. Domains are grouped per customer; your own domains (and, for a
Microsoft sign-in, Microsoft-owned domains) and consumer mail domains are never
treated as customers.

Nothing is linked silently. **Confirm customer** lets you name the customer,
adjust its domains and optionally link an MSX account from suggested candidates
with their confidence and evidence, or keep a local-only customer. A meeting can
also be marked **Not a customer meeting**. Associations are stored only in your
local Tomlook profile.

## Customer timeline

Open it from the meeting card, a company name or logo, or **Customers** in
Commands:

- **Meetings** with the customer from the local calendar (one month back, six
  months ahead), counts for the last 90 days, hours together and the next
  meeting;
- **Notes and decisions** and **tasks** from those meetings;
- **MSX**: open opportunities, active future milestones and whether you own a
  task for them; failed or truncated reads are never shown as "none";
- **Azure consumption**: monthly chart with completed months, the current
  partial month and model predictions drawn differently, unit and dataset;
- **Recent communication**: **Find related communication** runs one bounded
  AI search over the last 60 days of mail, Teams and meetings with the
  customer's people, focused on the selected meeting. Only links that were
  actually retrieved are shown as sources.

## MSX and Azure consumption connection

A new **MSX** status-bar entry connects to MSX Dataverse and the Azure
consumption Power BI model through their MCP servers, read-only, using your
Azure CLI sign-in for the Microsoft tenant. Tokens are kept in memory only.
Background checks never open a sign-in prompt; **Sign in to MSX** (browser or
Windows prompt) or **Use device code** start one only when you click them.
Signing in may change Azure CLI's default account. **Settings → MSX** can turn
the connection off.

Thorough Ask and meeting preparation can read MSX (one `SELECT` on six allowed
tables, at most 50 rows) and Azure consumption (one DAX `EVALUATE`, at most 200
rows) when connected. There is no write tool.

## Better meeting preparation for customer meetings

For confirmed customer meetings, preparation adds a bounded (4 KB) customer
block marked as untrusted data: MSX account, top opportunities, next milestones,
consumption trend and the last communication summary. It is fetched when a
meeting is prepared and does not by itself re-prepare existing meetings.
**Settings → MSX** can turn it off.

## Download and upgrade

v0.8.0 and later update automatically. Otherwise download
`Tomlook-v0.15.0-win-x64.zip`, fully exit Tomlook (including its tray
instance) and replace `Tomlook.exe`. Keep the existing `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tomlook`
profile.

## Verification scope

Offline tests cover domain detection, association and overrides, timeline
aggregation, MSX states, token handling and tenant verification, read-only
tool allowlisting, query validation, consumption labeling, source validation
for communication research and unchanged preparation fingerprints. The
timeline was checked visually in the offline demo with sample data. **MSX,
Power BI and the communication research have not yet been run against live
data**: they require an interactive MSX sign-in, which was not possible during
development.
