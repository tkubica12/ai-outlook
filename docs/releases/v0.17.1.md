# Tomlook v0.17.1

This patch adds the Teams meeting shortcut and makes category and
missing-material recommendations more conservative. These are changes since
[v0.17.0](https://github.com/tkubica12/ai-outlook/releases/tag/v0.17.0).

## Join Teams from the meeting menu

Right-click a meeting, press **Shift+F10**, or open **Meeting actions** and
choose **Join Teams meeting**. It uses the clicked meeting's saved join link
and the same opening and error handling as the detail pane.

The action appears for upcoming or ongoing meetings with a safe join link.
It does not need an AI or WorkIQ connection and never submits a calendar write.

## BLOCKER means uncertainty, not call

A `BLOCKER` title takes priority over an accepted invitation or a Teams link.
Automatic category recommendations and invitation defaults use the existing
`Nejisté`/`Nejiste` category, never fall back to `Blocker` or `Call`, and do not
invent a category if the uncertainty category is unavailable or ambiguous.
Explicit manual choices remain available.

The prompt and native recommendation checks enforce the same rule, including
an explanation based on the reservation rather than its online location.
Venue evidence is still considered independently for travel recommendations.

## Missing materials only when it matters

**Missing materials** is now an exceptional alert requiring all of:

- A confirmed meeting organized outside your organization.
- Evidence of a high-profile customer meeting with concrete stakes.
- Both the purpose of your attendance and your expected role still unknown.
- Completed mail and Teams research with no relevant context, including
  context already available in Insights.

A sparse invitation or missing attachment alone is not enough. BLOCKERs,
tentative reservations, internal corporate events and incomplete or
inaccessible research do not qualify. Structured assessment and captured
research evidence are checked before a recommendation enters Messages;
unsupported or ambiguous eligibility suppresses the alert.

Old pending category, travel and missing-material recommendations using
earlier policy rules expire. Uncertain write outcomes remain visible for
readback and are never replayed automatically.

## Download and upgrade

v0.8.0 and later can install this release after signed-update verification
when you choose **Restart now** in Settings -> Updates or exit Tomlook.
Otherwise download `Tomlook-v0.17.1-win-x64.zip`, fully exit Tomlook including
its tray instance and replace `Tomlook.exe`. Keep the existing
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tomlook` profile.

The release includes checksums, third-party notices and a signed update
manifest.

## Verification scope

Offline regression checks cover category precedence, invitation defaults,
rare-alert eligibility, source-access gaps, legacy recommendation expiry,
prompt/schema compatibility, worker admission and meeting-menu behavior.
An isolated demo startup checks the installed release binary without reading
the personal profile. These checks do not claim new live calendar writes or
live workplace-research acceptance.
