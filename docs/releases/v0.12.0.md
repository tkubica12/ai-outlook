# Tomlook v0.12.0

This release adds **My day** (**Můj den**), a local overview of today, and an
optional morning notification. These are changes since
[v0.11.0](https://github.com/tkubica12/ai-outlook/releases/tag/v0.11.0).
v0.8.0 and later install it automatically after verification when you choose
**Restart now** in Settings → Updates or exit Tomlook.

## My day

Open it from the new sunrise icon in the header, from Commands or with
**Ctrl+D**. Everything is computed from the saved calendar, tasks and notes;
opening it never calls AI or the network.

- **Summary:** meetings you organize or accepted, total meeting time, first
  start and last end; tentative meetings are counted separately.
- **Next meeting:** countdown, **Join** for a safe Teams link and **Open**.
- **Today's agenda** with Insights, notes, tentative and Teams badges.
- **Conflicts** between confirmed meetings (all-day items excluded).
- **Not prepared:** meetings without Insights, with **Research now** per
  meeting and **Research all**; disabled reasons are shown when AI or WorkIQ
  is not connected or automatic research is paused.
- **Missing materials** and **Waiting for approval**, linking to Messages.
- **Tasks:** overdue, due today and tasks imported from the previous
  workday's meeting notes (Friday to Sunday on Monday), with checkboxes.
- **Mentions of me** from the previous workday's notes.
- **Focus time:** free gaps of at least 30 minutes between 08:00 and 18:00,
  shown only when today's calendar is synchronized.
- **Tomorrow:** number of meetings and the first start.

## Morning notification

On workdays at 08:00 by default, Tomlook shows one Windows notification such
as "6 meetings · 1 conflict · 2 not prepared · 3 tasks" (Czech uses correct
plural forms). **Open** or a click opens My day. If Tomlook was not running or
the PC was asleep, the notification is shown at the first opportunity before
noon; otherwise that day is skipped. It stays quiet when today is not
synchronized or there is nothing to act on.

**Settings → Notifications → Morning overview** turns it on or off, sets the
time (07:00–10:00), includes weekends if wanted and offers **Show now** and
**Send test**.

## Fixes

- Czech status texts that end with a date, such as the last reminder time,
  are now translated.
- Mentions in My day no longer repeat the "Mention of me" heading.

## Download and upgrade

v0.8.0 and later update automatically. Otherwise download
`Tomlook-v0.12.0-win-x64.zip`, fully exit Tomlook (including its tray
instance) and replace `Tomlook.exe`. Keep the existing `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tomlook`
profile.

## Verification scope

Offline tests cover the overview rules (eligibility, conflicts, unprepared
meetings, previous-workday notes including Mondays, focus time with and
without synchronized coverage, tomorrow), Czech plurals and once-per-day
morning scheduling with the noon cut-off. The panel was checked visually in
the offline demo. The morning notification has not yet been observed live.
