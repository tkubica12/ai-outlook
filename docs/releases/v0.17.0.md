# Tomlook v0.17.0

This release adds approval-gated calendar creation and removal from Ask AI,
BLOCKER research, meeting-context shortcuts and categories when accepting an
invitation. These are changes since
[v0.16.0](https://github.com/tkubica12/ai-outlook/releases/tag/v0.16.0).
v0.8.0 and later install it automatically after verification when you choose
**Restart now** in Settings -> Updates or exit Tomlook.

## Ask AI calendar proposals

Ask AI can prepare a new timed event, including attendees, Teams, privacy,
category and optional travel blocks, or propose removing an obsolete
reservation. Review the exact proposal in **Messages** and approve it with
the corresponding button; chat cannot approve or execute a write.

Removal respects your role: delete your own appointment without attendees,
cancel a meeting you organize with notification to attendees, or decline an
invitation without notifying the organizer. Only the shown event or occurrence
is targeted, never an entire recurring series.

Approvals are durable, single-use and bound to the calendar account and event
fingerprint. Every write is checked against the current calendar and read back.
Uncertain outcomes require review and are never replayed automatically.

## BLOCKER research and meeting shortcuts

Use `/blocker` with a meeting context, or `/blocker <topic or person>`, to
research related reservations, possible final invitations, email and Teams
discussions. The prompt asks for dated original evidence, distinguishes missing
access from no matches, and suggests an evidence-backed contact and a draft
question when the reservation's status remains unclear. Research itself never
removes anything or sends a message.

Right-click a meeting, press **Shift+F10**, or open **Meeting actions**:

- **Check BLOCKER** starts `/blocker` for that meeting.
- **Prepare for this meeting** starts `/prepare` for that meeting.

Both commands go directly to **Thorough**, bypassing Auto, including when
typed. The clicked meeting supplies the context even if another meeting is
selected; unsent Ask drafts are preserved.

## Categories when accepting an invitation

The acceptance confirmation now offers category buttons with the same colored
backgrounds and selected outline as the new-event dialog, in light and dark
themes.

Fast deterministic rules can preselect an existing category from the cached
title, location and invitation preview, without AI or network calls:

| Clear evidence | Suggested existing category |
|---|---|
| Online meeting without a physical-location clue | Call |
| Your own or employer's office | Office |
| Customer/client premises | Mimo office |
| `BLOCKER` as a title word | Blocker, otherwise Nejisté |

Ambiguous or conflicting evidence gets no location-based guess. Existing
categories are preserved by default, and you can override or remove the
suggestion before confirming. The Follow marker is preserved.

One confirmation covers the chosen category and invitation response. The
category is assigned and verified before acceptance is submitted. If a later
step fails or remains uncertain, **Messages** explicitly reports the partial
result; checking it later only reads and never repeats either write.

## Other improvements

- The status bar shows the running Tomlook version.
- Graph event IDs containing `/`, `+` or `=` stay one encoded resource path
  segment for reads, writes and verification.
- Event creation preserves the exact approved time, including fractional
  seconds.
- Creation-proposal review keeps the intended proposal selected.
- Documentation is consolidated into shorter guides and reference pages;
  obsolete web-prototype files were removed.

## Download and upgrade

v0.8.0 and later update automatically. Otherwise download
`Tomlook-v0.17.0-win-x64.zip`, fully exit Tomlook (including its tray instance)
and replace `Tomlook.exe`. Keep the existing `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tomlook` profile.
The release includes checksums and a signed update manifest.

## Verification scope

Offline regression and native UI checks cover typed proposals, explicit
single-use approvals, account and stale-event guards, BLOCKER prompts,
right-click context and draft preservation, direct Thorough routing, category
rules, matching category colors, compound acceptance and read-only recovery.
These checks do not claim a new live calendar-write or live workplace-research
acceptance run.
