# Tomlook v0.19.0

This release makes customer confirmation a one-click decision based on your
MSX territory, adds partners and untracked organizations, and keeps WorkIQ
connected after sleep or an expired sign-in. These are changes since
[v0.18.2](https://github.com/tkubica12/ai-outlook/releases/tag/v0.18.2).

## Customers: suggestions from your MSX territory

Unconfirmed external domains of a meeting are now grouped by organization.
Domains with the same distinctive name, such as `drmax.eu`, `drmax.sk` and
`dr-max.global`, form one card. Its name comes from attendee affiliations such
as "(Dr.Max SVK)" when they agree.

With MSX connected, Tomlook looks up each organization in the background
(read-only) and shows a suggestion:

- Accounts in your MSX territory, the accounts whose account team you are on,
  are preferred.
- An account outside the territory that shares the MSX top parent (TPID) of a
  territory account is represented by that territory account. For example,
  Dr.Max suggests **PENTA INVESTMENTS**.
- **Confirm Dr.Max → PENTA INVESTMENTS** saves the customer in one click.
  Nothing is linked before you click.

**Choose customer...** opens the dialog with candidates, their confidence and
evidence, and a filterable list of your territory. You can also keep a local
customer without an MSX link.

A new domain of a saved customer, for example another country, offers
**Add to <customer>**.

## Partners and untracked organizations

- **It's a partner** remembers a partner or vendor locally, with its logo and
  without an MSX link. It is shown as a partner and never offered as a
  customer again. Partners add no customer context to meeting preparation.
- **Don't track** hides an organization's domains for good.

Both choices appear in **Customers**, where they can be edited or removed.

## WorkIQ stays connected

When a reconnect failed, for example because the network was not up yet after
sleep, Tomlook classified the failure as permanent and stopped retrying until
you clicked **Sign in**. Network failures during discovery, metadata reads and
token refreshes now retry automatically with backoff from 30 seconds to at
most 15 minutes.

If Microsoft Entra rejects the saved sign-in, Tomlook opens browser sign-in
for the selected account by itself, at most once per hour. With Windows
single sign-on this usually completes without clicking. **Disconnect** stops
it.

## Download and upgrade

v0.8.0 and later can install this release after signed-update verification
when you choose **Restart now** in Settings -> Updates or exit Tomlook.
Otherwise download `Tomlook-v0.19.0-win-x64.zip`, fully exit Tomlook including
its tray instance and replace `Tomlook.exe`. Keep the existing
`%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tomlook` profile.

Existing customers keep their stored format. The release includes checksums,
third-party notices and a signed update manifest.

## Verification scope

Offline tests cover:

- organization grouping, partner and untracked detection, and stored-format
  compatibility;
- territory ranking and merging accounts with the same top parent;
- the background suggestion and one-click confirmation;
- the territory picker and the bilingual labels;
- classification of transient WorkIQ failures and the hourly limit on
  automatic browser sign-in.

Clippy, app tests and integration tests passed. The territory query was
checked read-only against live MSX. A live WorkIQ reconnect after sleep was
not reproduced as part of this verification.

This release does not claim a full-library-suite pass. Four library tests
already failed before these changes: three preparation-prompt length tests
documented in v0.18.1 and one worker recommendation refresh test.
