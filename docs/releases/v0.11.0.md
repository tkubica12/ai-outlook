# Tomlook v0.11.0

This release closes the loop after a meeting: tasks from meeting notes land in
a local task list, and a follow-up e-mail can be prepared from the notes.
These are changes since
[v0.10.0](https://github.com/tkubica12/ai-outlook/releases/tag/v0.10.0).
v0.8.0 and later install it automatically after verification when you choose
**Restart now** in Settings → Updates or exit Tomlook.

## Local task list

A new **Tasks** (**Úkoly**) panel opens from the header icon (with an
open-task count) or from Commands (**Open tasks**, **Add task**).

- Open tasks are grouped as overdue, due today, upcoming and without a date;
  completed tasks from the last 30 days stay in a collapsed list.
- Each task can be completed or reopened, renamed, given or cleared a due date,
  deleted with Undo, and opened at its source meeting.
- Personal tasks from post-meeting notes are added automatically when the
  notes are ready (and once for notes from the last seven days). The title uses
  the interface language; the other language is kept in the task note. A due
  date is set only when the item names one explicit date with a year.
- A deleted or completed imported task never comes back, even if the notes
  are edited. Mentions and talking points are not turned into tasks.
- Ended meeting details list that meeting's tasks with checkboxes right below
  **My tasks**.

The list is stored only on this computer. Nothing is written to Microsoft To
Do, Planner or Dataverse.

## Follow-up e-mail

Ended meetings with notes offer **Prepare follow-up e-mail**
(**Připravit follow-up e-mail**). One explicit, tool-free Quick request drafts
a subject and message from the notes, decisions, your tasks, open questions
and shared links, marking uncertain items for confirmation.

- Choose recipients (attendees and organizer; you, your other mailboxes and
  rooms are excluded), edit the subject and message, then **Copy** or
  **Open in Outlook**, which opens a `mailto:` message in your mail app.
- Very long messages open with recipients and subject only; the body is copied
  to the clipboard for pasting.
- **Regenerate** and **Discard** are available; the last draft is kept per
  meeting until discarded.

Tomlook never sends e-mail and never creates Outlook drafts. A pending
follow-up recommendation links directly to this section.

## Download and upgrade

v0.8.0 and later update automatically. Otherwise download
`Tomlook-v0.11.0-win-x64.zip`, fully exit Tomlook (including its tray
instance) and replace `Tomlook.exe`. Keep the existing `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tomlook`
profile; the task and follow-up tables are added automatically.

## Verification scope

Offline tests cover task storage, completion, deletion tombstones, notes
import deduplication across restarts and revisions, language selection,
explicit due dates, removal of note headings from task titles, `mailto:`
encoding with diacritics and the long-message fallback, recipient selection
and the bounded follow-up request. The panel and follow-up draft were checked
visually in the offline demo. A live follow-up draft has not yet been
generated with a signed-in Copilot account.
