# Tomlook v0.13.0

This release adds a **Weekly review** (**Týdenní přehled**). These are changes
since [v0.12.0](https://github.com/tkubica12/ai-outlook/releases/tag/v0.12.0).
v0.8.0 and later install it automatically after verification when you choose
**Restart now** in Settings → Updates or exit Tomlook.

## Weekly review

Open it from Commands, from the bottom of My day, or with the **Weekly
review** button next to the date range in Week and Work week views. Weeks run
Monday to Sunday; arrows browse earlier weeks. Everything is computed locally.

- **Headline numbers:** meetings attended (tentative separately), meeting time
  (overlaps count once), average per workday, share of Mon–Fri 08:00–18:00,
  focus time and the longest free block, each compared with the average of the
  previous four sufficiently synchronized weeks.
- **Time by category** with the category colors; each meeting counts in full
  toward its first category.
- **Conflicts:** confirmed overlaps, total overlapping time and the pairs.
- **Meetings without an agenda:** split into meetings you organize (add an
  agenda so participants can prepare) and meetings others organize.
- **Meeting load:** back-to-back chains, days with more than 6 hours of
  meetings, short and long meetings.
- **People and customers:** who you spent the most meeting time with and which
  external companies (by domain); you, your other mailboxes and rooms are
  excluded.
- **Preparation and follow-through:** Insights and meeting-notes coverage,
  tasks created and completed.
- **Copy summary** copies a short text summary.
- **Summarize the week with AI** (explicit, one tool-free Quick request) turns
  the numbers and the week's meeting notes into highlights, decisions, open
  loops and suggestions, using only that data. The last summary per week is
  kept locally.

Days that are not synchronized are listed and the numbers are marked partial;
unknown time is never counted as free.

## Agenda detection

The calendar sync now also reads each meeting's short description preview
(`bodyPreview`, at most 255 characters). The Teams join block, dial-in
numbers and links are ignored; a meeting needs real text to count as having an
agenda. Meetings cached before this version show as **not checked** until their
day is refreshed (within 15 minutes for the next two weeks, otherwise within
6 hours).

The preview is local-only: it does not change any meeting's identity, does not
expire recommendations or approvals, does not re-trigger AI preparation and is
not added to AI preparation prompts.

## Weekly notification

On Friday at 15:00 by default, one Windows notification summarizes the week,
for example "18 meetings · 22 h · 3 conflicts · 4 without agenda"; **Open**
shows the review. Monday mode reviews the previous week instead. It appears
once per week, catches up until the end of that day and only when at least
four of five workdays are synchronized. **Settings → Notifications → Weekly
review** controls it.

## Download and upgrade

v0.8.0 and later update automatically. Otherwise download
`Tomlook-v0.13.0-win-x64.zip`, fully exit Tomlook (including its tray
instance) and replace `Tomlook.exe`. Keep the existing `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Tomlook`
profile.

## Verification scope

Offline tests cover the metric definitions, overlap handling, focus time,
conflicts, agenda detection on English and Czech Teams previews cut at every
length, people and company aggregation, coverage-based trends, DST weeks,
notification scheduling and unchanged preparation fingerprints with the new
preview field. The panel was checked visually in the offline demo. The live
preview sync and the AI week summary have not yet been observed with a
signed-in account.
